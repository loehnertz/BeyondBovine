# 0004 — A learning record in `docs/LEARNING.md`

**Status:** Accepted by Jakob, 2026-09-24

## Context

Jakob wants a learning approach that carries across sessions without re-explaining it each time. He also wants his curiosity-driven questions to survive branching. [Decision 0002](0002-project-records.md) had no place for either.

## Decision

Add `docs/LEARNING.md` to the project records. It extends 0002. It holds:

- the agreed learning approach and style
- the current learning goal
- the topics covered, each with what Jakob himself has said about it
- parked questions

## Rules

- Record what Jakob stated or predicted, and how it compared with the explanation. Record agent explanations only as a list of topics covered.
- Material deliberately withheld for Jakob to discover may be recorded for agents inside a collapsed spoiler block.
- Parked questions stay on the list until they are answered or Jakob drops them.
- `docs/STATE.md` links here and does not copy the contents.
